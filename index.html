BeepedBox Beta

BeepedBox is BeepBox, remastered: the same sketch-an-instrumental-song layout, with a much bigger instrument list and patterns that never share edits.

Instructions

Click the gray rows at the top to add notes; click a note to remove it, drag sideways on a note to change its length. Click above or below a note to stack more notes (chords).

The numbered boxes at the bottom are the patterns of each row. Click a box to select it. Click the top half of the selected box to move to the next pattern, the bottom half for the previous one. Unlike BeepBox, editing a pattern that appears in several bars only changes the bar you are editing, so two blocks never edit each other. (Turn on Edit Shared Patterns Together in Preferences if you want the classic behaviour.)

Tools: Draw, Select, Bend, Erase, Paint, Split, Join, Chord, Slide, Volume (keys W A B E P T G H L F). ＋ Layer adds a channel from a genre of instruments (drums, bass, strings, OS sounds and more). Export saves .mp3 .wav .flac .ogg .opus .aac .m4a .shn .dsf .dff .midi or a .beeped project; Import loads a .beeped or .json song.

The purple loop controls which part of the song repeats; drag it or its ends. Keys: Space play/pause, Z undo, Y redo, C/V copy/paste pattern, 0-9 set pattern number, arrows move the selection, + / - transpose notes.
